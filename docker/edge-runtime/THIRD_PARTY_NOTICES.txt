Qor edge runtime third-party notices
====================================

The authenticated edge-runtime archive contains unmodified or Qor-patched
binary distributions of the following components. The archive manifest records
the hashes of the distributed binaries and libraries.

HAProxy 3.2.21
  Source: https://www.haproxy.org/download/3.2/src/haproxy-3.2.21.tar.gz
  License: GPL-2.0-or-later, with the OpenSSL linking exception described by
  HAProxy's LICENSE file, exportable headers are LGPL-2.1 or later.

Tor 0.4.9.11, with Qor's hidden-service descriptor publication patch
  Source: https://dist.torproject.org/tor-0.4.9.11.tar.gz
  Patch: docker/tor/patches/0001-retry-hsdesc-upload.patch
  License: BSD-3-Clause and other permissive licenses detailed in Tor's LICENSE.

liboqs 0.16.0
  Source: https://github.com/open-quantum-safe/liboqs/tree/0.16.0
  License: MIT, with separately licensed implementations detailed by liboqs.

Open Quantum Safe OpenSSL provider 0.12.0-dev (dfefe2d)
  Source: https://github.com/open-quantum-safe/oqs-provider/tree/dfefe2d
  License: MIT.

The private OpenSSL, zlib, libevent, xz/liblzma, zstd, PCRE2, and libxcrypt
runtime libraries originate from the pinned Ubuntu 22.04 build base. Their
copyright and license records are available from the corresponding Ubuntu
source packages.

Qor's source tree retains the exact Tor patch, source archive hashes, runtime
archive hash, and per-file binary hashes needed to identify this distribution.
